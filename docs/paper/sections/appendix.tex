\section{Wire Formats}
\label{app:formats}

All integers are little-endian. \code{str} is a 2-byte length followed by UTF-8 bytes. Every format begins with a 4-byte magic and a 1-byte version, no two formats share a magic, and decoders reject unknown versions and trailing bytes.
