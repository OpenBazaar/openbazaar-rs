\section{Wire Formats}
\label{app:formats}

All integers are little-endian. \code{str} is a 2-byte length followed by UTF-8 bytes. Every format begins with a 4-byte magic and a 1-byte version, and decoders reject unknown versions and trailing bytes.

\code{OBST} v2 & Store & name (str), region (str), BOLT 12 offer (str), BIP 352 code (str), store key (32 bytes), store rating (u8: 0 general, 1 sensitive, 2 adult), then a 64-byte BIP 340 signature over the preceding bytes with tag \code{ob/store/v1}. Version 1 omits the rating. The mailbox key and onion address of \S\ref{sec:identity} are added at the end in a later version. \\
\code{OBLS} v5 & Listing & store key (32), title (str), category (str), price (u64), ship-from (str), description (str), seller (str), stock (u32), listed-on date (u32), moderated (u8), kind (u8), product (str), shipping (u64), free-over (u64), rating (u8), tags (u16 bit set), then a signature with tag \code{ob/listing/v1}. Address: SHA-256 of everything before the signature. Version 4 omits the rating and tags. \\
\code{OBLB} v1 & Label set & labeler key (32), name (str, at most 64 bytes), sequence (u64), issued-on date (u32), entry count (u16, at most 4{,}096), then entries sorted by target with no repeats: target kind (u8: 0 listing, 1 store), target (32), flags (u8; bit 0 retracts, others zero), rating (u8), tags (u16), reason (str, \code{[a-z0-9-]}, at most 32 bytes). A retraction has rating 0, tags 0, and no reason. Signature with tag \code{ob/labels/v1}; address: SHA-256 of the body. \\
\code{OBOD} v1 & Order plaintext & listing address (32), quantity (u32), option count (u16) and name/value pairs (str, str), ship-to (str), rail (u8), reply key (32). \\
\code{OBSL} v1 & Sealed letter & HPKE encapsulated key (32), ciphertext length (u32), ciphertext. At most 64~KiB. Orders and escrow messages use different HPKE \code{info} strings, so neither opens as the other. \\
\code{OBSR} v1 & Sealed reply & As \code{OBSL}, sealed to the order's reply key; at most 8~KiB. \\
\code{OBML} v1 & Onion frame & Letter length (u32), then one \code{OBSL} letter. \\
\code{OBET} v2 & Escrow terms & The keys, moderator record (keys, payout, fee, policy), amounts, heights, and payout scripts of \S\ref{sec:escrow-notation}. \\
\code{OBEM} v1 & Escrow mail & Message kind (u8), escrow id (16), field count (u8), then fields as u32 length and bytes; sealed as an \code{OBSL} letter between nodes. \\
\code{OBDL} v3 & Dispute letter & The fields of the dispute letter in \S\ref{sec:escrow}, then the claimant's signature. \\
\code{OBRU} v2 & Ruling & Terms digest (32), output key (32), funding outpoint, favored party (u8), adaptor point (33), moderator share (32), signature (64) by the moderator's signing key over all preceding fields. \\
